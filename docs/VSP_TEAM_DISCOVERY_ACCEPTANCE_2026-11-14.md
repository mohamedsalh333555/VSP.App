# Team discovery and details navigation — 15.01–15.03

The rankings tab already reads `teams_public` via `TeamRepository.getTeams()` and renders team cards, a search field, and governorate/sport filters. A new widget test feeds two synthetic teams into the actual tab. It verifies that an Aswan football filter displays the matching team and hides a Cairo basketball team, that an unmatched query empties the visible list, that a matching query restores the card, and that changing the filters hides both. A second test taps the card and checks that the selected team ID reaches the team-profile route. The optional builder used in that test leaves the normal `TeamProfileScreen` route in place.

[Flutter CI 43642905483](https://github.com/mohamedsalh333555/VSP.App/actions/runs/43642905483) runs `flutter analyze` and the full test suite, including this widget test and the stadium-details transition test. These checks establish loaded-list presentation, local filtering/search and route selection. They do not establish server-side full pagination (the repository currently limits its query to 50), live public-team data, a real JWT, private-team visibility, join eligibility or a device journey. Tasks 15.05 and 15.06 remain open.
